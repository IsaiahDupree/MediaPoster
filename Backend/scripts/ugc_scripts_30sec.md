## Recording Tips

1. **Lighting:** Natural light or ring light facing you
2. **Framing:** Head and shoulders, centered
3. **Energy:** Conversational but enthusiastic
4. **Pacing:** ~2.5 words per second for 30s
5. **Eye contact:** Look at camera lens, not screen
6. **Multiple takes:** Get 3-5 takes, pick best energy

## Platform Optimization

- **TikTok:** Post as-is, strong hook critical
- **Instagram Reels:** Add captions for silent viewing
- **YouTube Shorts:** Thumbnail matters less, hook is key
