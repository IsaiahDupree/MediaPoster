## Project Overview
MediaPoster is a comprehensive autonomous content ops controller with:
- Safari automation for browser-based social posting
- Multi-platform publishing (X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads)
- Media factory (Sora video generation, Remotion, AI characters, music)
- Narrative scheduling with FATE framework (Focus, Authority, Tribe, Emotion)
- Sleep/Wake mode for CPU efficiency
- Experiments and A/B testing system
- Content pipeline with auto-sourcing, analysis, and curation
- Multi-channel engagement (comments, DMs, email loops)
